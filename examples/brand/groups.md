# Audience Groups

One row per group that appears in `leads/leads.csv`. The `personalize` skill reads this file to shape the opening line and example for each group.

| Group | Who they are |
|---|---|
| `freelancers` | Solo operators focused on time. Lead with how fast they can get started. Speak to the cost of context-switching and the value of getting it done without a team. |
| `agency-owners` | Manage multiple clients. Lead with control and visibility across accounts. Speak to client reporting, approval workflows, and protecting margins. |
| `in-house-teams` | Work inside larger orgs. Lead with how this fits existing approval workflows. Speak to stakeholder buy-in, audit trails, and not rocking the boat. |

# Brand Voice

## Tone (three words)

Clear. Warm. Direct.

## Words and phrases to avoid

- synergy, leverage, circle back, reach out, touch base
- game-changer, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, best-in-class
- "I wanted to", "I hope this finds you well"
- exclamation marks more than once per email

## Claims you must never make

- Guaranteed results or outcomes
- "Industry-leading" or "market-leading" without a cited source
- Specific time-to-value numbers unless they come from a case study

## Sign-off

End every email with:

```
Cheers,
[Sender first name]
[Title], [Company]
```
